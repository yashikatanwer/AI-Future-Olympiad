# 🤖 AI Future Olympiad (AIFO)

### AI Awareness & Learning Platform for Grades 5–8

AI Future Olympiad (AIFO) is a proposed school-focused AI awareness and learning program designed to help students understand Artificial Intelligence, think critically about AI, and use technology responsibly.

## 💡 About the Initiative

I created AIFO as a proposed school program to introduce students to Artificial Intelligence through age-appropriate learning, interactive activities, guided practice, and responsible technology use, followed by a proposed AI Olympiad.

As AI is becoming increasingly relevant in everyday life, education, and future careers, students can benefit from understanding what AI is, how it works at a basic level, where it can make mistakes, and how to use it responsibly. A proposed AI Olympiad can provide a structured way to reinforce this learning while encouraging curiosity, reasoning, and future readiness.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Build awareness of Artificial Intelligence in everyday life
- Develop curiosity and logical thinking
- Help students understand how AI learns and makes predictions
- Encourage responsible and safe use of AI
- Introduce students to future opportunities in technology
