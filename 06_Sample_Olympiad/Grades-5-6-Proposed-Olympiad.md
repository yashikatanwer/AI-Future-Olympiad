# AIFO Proposed Olympiad — Grades 5–6
**Status:** Sample / proposed assessment  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Suggested total:** 20 marks

## Section A — MCQs
1. Which is a common example of AI? A. Voice assistant B. Pencil C. Ruler D. Paper
2. A map application may suggest a route because it can use: A. Road information B. Random number only C. Printed book D. Nothing
3. A recommendation is best understood as: A. Guaranteed answer B. Prediction or suggestion C. Command D. Unchangeable fact
4. If an AI gives a surprising answer, you should: A. Trust it B. Check it C. Share it immediately D. Ignore everything else
5. Which is a responsible AI habit? A. Share private information B. Check important outputs C. Copy everything D. Believe every result

## Section B — Reasoning
6. A student watches three football videos and receives more football recommendations. Give one reason.
7. A voice assistant misunderstands a student's question. Give one possible reason.
8. Explain “learning from examples” in your own words.

## Section C — Scenario-Based
9. Your friend says, “AI said it, so it must be true.” What would you tell them?
10. You want to use an AI tool for a school activity. Name two responsible things you should do.
