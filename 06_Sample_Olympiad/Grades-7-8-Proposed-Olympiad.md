# AIFO Proposed Olympiad — Grades 7–8
**Status:** Sample / proposed assessment  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Suggested total:** 25 marks

## Section A — MCQs
1. A recommendation system primarily tries to: A. Predict what a user may prefer B. Guarantee a choice C. Eliminate human decisions D. Work without information
2. Learning from examples is useful because: A. Patterns can be identified B. Machines become human C. Every example is perfect D. Errors become impossible
3. An AI output may be wrong because: A. Inputs/examples or the system can be limited B. AI cannot process information C. AI never uses patterns D. Human checking is unnecessary
4. Which is strongest responsible-use practice? A. Verify important information B. Share private data C. Copy answers D. Assume confidence means correctness
5. A recommendation can be influenced by: A. Previous activity or preferences B. Nothing C. Only weather D. A fixed answer

## Section B — Reasoning
6. Explain why two users may receive different video recommendations.
7. Give one way poor examples could affect an AI system.
8. Why should people remain involved in an important AI-assisted decision?

## Section C — Scenario-Based
9. An AI answer conflicts with a reliable source. What should a student do?
10. A student uploads another person's private information without permission. What is the concern?
11. A school allows AI brainstorming but wants students to learn and produce their own work. Suggest two responsible practices.
12. A recommendation system repeatedly shows one type of content. What problem could this create?
