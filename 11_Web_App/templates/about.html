About AIFO

AI Future Olympiad (AIFO) is a proposed school-ready AI awareness and Olympiad program for Grades 5–8.

The program is designed around three phases: Awareness, Guided Learning and Olympiad Assessment.

Important: This portfolio implementation represents the designed/proposed program. It does not claim that school sessions or an Olympiad were actually conducted.
