# AIFO Teacher / Facilitator Guide

## Phase 1 — Awareness
Suggested flow:
- 10 min: warm-up — where students see AI
- 15 min: what AI is
- 15 min: humans vs machines
- 15 min: AI around us
- 10 min: responsible AI
- 15 min: interactive activity

Use age-appropriate examples. Do not present AI as magic or as a perfect decision-maker.

## Phase 2 — Guided Learning
Use the relevant student booklet over the proposed 2–3 weeks. Pair concepts with activities, practice and reflection.

## Phase 3 — Proposed Olympiad
The original proposal describes a 60-minute assessment with MCQs, reasoning and scenario-based questions. The papers in this repository are samples and should be finalized before real implementation.

## Checklist
- Proposal shared
- Collaboration confirmed
- Dates agreed
- Student groups identified
- Awareness session prepared
- Activities prepared
- Student materials distributed
- Practice completed
- Assessment selected
- Answer key secured
- Feedback prepared

Do not add school participation, student counts, outcomes, photos or results until they actually exist.
